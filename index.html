🎉 Office Games

Monthly social hour — bring your A-game.

🎨

Pictionary

Red vs Blue. Draw, guess, argue about whether that's a horse or a dog.
📞

Drawing Telephone

Write → draw → describe → draw. Watch your sentence mutate into nonsense.
🧠

Quiz Buzzer

Host asks, teams race to buzz. Answer out loud, argue about the adjudication.
🕵️

Codenames

One-word clues, 25 words, one assassin. Don't click the assassin.
🤥

Fibbage

Weird true facts. Write a convincing lie, then try to spot the truth.
🎬

Emoji Guesser

Translate movies, songs and sayings into emoji. Teams race to decode.
🟩

Wordle Race

Same word, two teams, six guesses. You see their colors, not their letters.
🌍

Polyglot

Perro, chien, hund — match the words that mean the same thing, fastest team wins.
🐸

Frogger Rush

Everyone hops at once. Cross the road, dodge the traffic, shame the drowned.
🎤

Finish the Line

"Bohemian ___" — fill in the missing word before the other team does.
